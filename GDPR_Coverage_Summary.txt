CRITICAL GAPS: 5 ARTICLES NEED CONTROL DESCRIPTIONS
================================================================================

Priority 1 - OPERATIONAL CONTROLS NEEDED:

  1. Art 31 - Cooperation with Supervisory Authority
     └─ Current Status: Not covered
     └─ Should be added to: Control I.1 or I.4 (Breach Management)
     └─ What's needed: Procedure for responding to authority requests, 
                       providing information, permitting inspections

  2. Art 36 - Prior Consultation
     └─ Current Status: Not covered
     └─ Should be added to: Control B.2 (Risk Assessment)
     └─ What's needed: Process for consulting on high-risk processing,
                       DPIA involvement, pre-processing assessment

  3. Art 12 Enhancement - Transparent Information & Modalities
     └─ Current Status: Partially covered in H.1
     └─ Enhancement needed: More detail on transparency mechanisms
     └─ What's needed: Clear language, accessibility, understandability,
                       concise presentation, readily accessible information

  4. Art 19 Enhancement - Notification of Rectification/Erasure
     └─ Current Status: Partially in H.2
     └─ Enhancement needed: Add recipient notification requirement
     └─ What's needed: Procedure to notify third parties who received data
                       when data is corrected or deleted

  5. Art 48 - Unlawful Transfers/Disclosures
     └─ Current Status: Not covered
     └─ Should be added to: Control A.3 (Unlawful Instructions)
     └─ What's needed: Procedure for refusing unlawful requests from
                       authorities or third parties

SCOPE GAPS: 12 ARTICLES NOT IN ISAE PROCESSOR SCOPE
================================================================================

CONTROLLER RESPONSIBILITIES (Not Processor Auditable):
  • Art 13-14: Information provision to data subjects
  • Art 21-22: Rights to object/automated decisions
  • Art 26-27: Joint controller arrangement & representative

OPTIONAL (Based on Organization):
  • Art 37-39: Data Protection Officer (only if required or appointed)

DATA GOVERNANCE (Policy/Classification, Not Procedure):
  • Art 7-11: Consent, special categories, criminal data

REGULATORY (Not Operational Control):
  • Art 83: Administrative fines and penalties

ASSESSMENT: These 12 articles are correctly OUT of scope for an ISAE 3000 
processor audit. They are either controller responsibility, optional, or 
regulatory compliance (not operational control).
