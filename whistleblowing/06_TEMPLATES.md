# Whistleblowing Templates

**Data & More ApS**
**Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** Compliance Lead
**Review:** Annually

Use these templates as written, replacing the bracketed fields. Do not add detail that is not needed. Do not send case information to a personal mailbox or an unsecured channel.

---

## Template A — Acknowledgment of receipt

**Send within 7 calendar days of receipt.**

> **Subject:** Acknowledgment of your report — case [CASE ID]
>
> Dear [Name],
>
> Thank you for your report received on [date]. This is to confirm that Data & More has received it and opened case [CASE ID].
>
> **What happens next**
>
> - We will assess the concern and check for conflicts by [date — within 5 working days].
> - We will investigate proportionately and keep you informed.
> - We will give you feedback by [date — within 3 months of this acknowledgment], or explain why we need more time.
>
> Your identity will be kept confidential as far as the law allows. We will share it only with people who need it to handle the case, unless you consent or the law requires disclosure.
>
> You are protected from retaliation. If you believe you have been treated worse because of this report, tell [contact] immediately.
>
> If you have additional information, or if you want to report to the National Whistleblower Scheme instead, you can contact whistleblower.dk or 33 19 32 02.
>
> Kind regards,
> [Name]
> [Role]

## Template D — Non-retaliation reminder to managers

**Send to any manager who manages the reporter, or who may be asked to make a decision affecting them.**

> **Subject:** Confidential — case [CASE ID]
>
> You are receiving this because [name] has made a report. You are not being asked to investigate.
>
> Please:
>
> - keep the matter confidential;
> - make no change to [name]'s duties, pay, shifts, training, reviews or opportunities without a documented business reason approved by [decision-maker];
> - tell [Compliance Lead] before any management action affecting [name];
> - report any concern about retaliation immediately.
>
> This is a legal obligation under the Danish Whistleblower Act. Do not discuss this with the reporter or with colleagues.
>
> [Compliance Lead]
