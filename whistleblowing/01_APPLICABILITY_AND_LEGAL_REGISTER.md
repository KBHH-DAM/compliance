## 1. Decision

Data & More ApS operates a **voluntary, lightweight whistleblowing scheme**. It does **not** operate a statutory internal whistleblower scheme under §9 of the Act, because the company is below the 50-employee threshold.

Reports are routed primarily to the **National Whistleblower Scheme** at Datatilsynet (`whistleblower.dk`). An optional internal **Speak-Up Contact** is available for employees who want to talk to someone before deciding whether to report.

## 2. Legal basis and threshold

| Point | Position |
|---|---|
| §9(1) | Employers with **50 or more employees** must establish an internal whistleblower scheme. Data & More is below 50 and is not captured. |
| §9(2) | The Minister can extend the duty to smaller employers by order after a risk assessment. No sector order has been identified as applicable; confirm at each review. |
| §9(3) | Group-common schemes are permitted. If Data & More is part of a group with a whistleblowing scheme, review the group scheme before relying on it. |
| §31(2) | The transition for private employers with 50–249 employees ended on **17 December 2023**. There is no transition period if the threshold is crossed now. |
| §§5–8, 28–29 | Protection against retaliation, confidentiality, compensation and the reversed burden of proof apply **regardless of employer size**. |
| §17 | Datatilsynet operates the National Whistleblower Scheme. Employees can report there directly. |

**Headcount evidence:** 27 employees recorded in `DESCRIPTION_OF_PROCESSING_ISAE3000.md` as of June 2025. Confirm current headcount at approval and at each annual review, and before any hiring plan that could cross 45.

## 3. What applies today

Even though the §9 scheme duty does not apply:

1. Employees may report to `whistleblower.dk` without going through Data & More first.
2. Data & More must not retaliate, threaten retaliation or obstruct a report (§8).
3. If an employee proves they reported and suffered a detriment, Data & More must prove the detriment was not retaliation (§29).
4. A dismissal in breach of §8 must be overturned and the employment maintained or restored if the employee wishes, unless clearly unreasonable (§28(2)).
5. Compensation is available for retaliation (§28).
6. Confidentiality of the reporter, the accused and third parties must be protected in any handling Data & More carries out.
7. Personal data in a report must be processed only as necessary, secured and retained only as long as necessary.

## 4. Why a voluntary scheme

- It gives staff a clear, safe route and reduces the risk of a report being mishandled by a manager.
- It meets the §12 handling standards early, so the company is ready at 50.
- It provides audit evidence for A.5.31, A.5.37 and A.5.36.
- It satisfies customer due-diligence questionnaires that ask for a speak-up channel.
- It does not create the full administrative burden of a statutory scheme.

## 5. Trigger points

| Trigger | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Headcount reaches **45** | Review the scheme, budget for a full §9 implementation, draft the upgrade plan | Management |
| Headcount reaches **50** | Establish a full §9 internal scheme **before** the threshold is crossed. No grace period. Appoint an impartial unit, publish the procedure, meet §12 deadlines | Management |
| A customer contract or tender requires an internal scheme | Treat as mandatory regardless of headcount | Management |
| A group company operates a §9 scheme | Consider joining the group scheme under §9(3) | Management |
| A sector order under §9(2) applies | Implement the full scheme | Management |
| A material amendment to the Act | Review this decision and the policy | Compliance Lead |
