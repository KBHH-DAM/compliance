## 1. Why this pack exists

Data & More ApS is below the 50-employee threshold in §9 of the Danish Whistleblower Act (Act No. 1436 of 29 June 2021), so it is **not required** to operate a statutory internal whistleblower scheme.

The Act's **protection rules still apply**. Employees, contractors, interns, former staff and applicants can report wrongdoing to the National Whistleblower Scheme, and Data & More must not retaliate.

This pack gives Data & More a **lightweight, voluntary scheme** that:

- routes reports to the independent National Whistleblower Scheme (`whistleblower.dk`);
- gives employees an optional internal contact if they want to talk to someone first;
- meets the handling standards in §12 of the Act (7-day acknowledgment, 3-month feedback, confidentiality, impartial handling) even though they are not yet mandatory;
- produces the evidence needed for ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.31, A.5.37 and A.5.36;
- scales into a full §9 scheme when the company reaches 50 employees.

## 4. Implementation — five steps

1. **Confirm the trigger.** Current headcount, group structure and customer contract requirements. Record the conclusion in the legal register.
2. **Approve.** Management signs the policy and the applicability decision.
3. **Configure.** Create the case folder, register, shared mailbox or alias, and access list. Do not use a personal mailbox.
4. **Publish and train.** Add the handbook section, brief managers, run a 30-minute all-staff session, file the attendance record.
5. **Test.** Run one tabletop case from receipt to closure. Fix anything that does not work. Set the annual review date.

## 5. How to use this pack in an audit

For ISO/IEC 27001:2022, the pack is evidence for:

- **A.5.31** — legal and regulatory requirements identified and assessed;
- **A.5.37** — documented operating procedure;
- **A.5.36** — compliance monitored and reviewed.

It is **not** one of the 45 ISAE 3000 controls and should **not** be added to Elumi's 99-document request list.

## 6. Design principles

- **External-first.** The primary route is the independent National Whistleblower Scheme. No one needs permission to use it.
- **Optional internal contact.** The Speak-Up Contact helps people navigate the scheme; they do not run a parallel investigation alongside the National Scheme.
- **Build to §12 even though it is not yet mandatory.** The scheme meets the statutory handling standard, so it is defensible now and ready at 50.
- **Minimal data.** The register holds the least data needed to track a case.
- **Separate files.** Whistleblowing records stay separate from HR, security incident and customer complaint files.
- **No retaliation.** The policy, manager briefing and 30/90/180-day checks make the protection operational, not just a statement.
