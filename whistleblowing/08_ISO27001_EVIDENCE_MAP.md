## 2. What to give an auditor

1. This pack, approved and version-controlled.
2. The legal register entry (A.5.31).
3. The policy and procedure.
4. The case register (redacted).
5. One sample case file from receipt to closure (redacted).
6. Training records.
7. The annual review and metrics.
8. The DPIA screening.

## 3. What not to do

- Do not add this pack to Elumi's 99-document ISAE 3000 request list. It is not one of the 45 ISAE controls.
- Do not describe the scheme as a statutory §9 scheme while the company is below 50.
- Do not claim that a report was investigated by Data & More if it was handled by the National Scheme.
- Do not put the case register in a shared drive or a personal mailbox.
- Do not keep case data longer than the retention rule allows.

## 5. Sample audit questions and prepared answers

| Question | Answer | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Are you required to operate a whistleblowing scheme? | Not under §9, because we are below 50 employees. We operate a voluntary scheme and we comply with the protection rules | Applicability decision |
| How can an employee report? | Directly to the National Whistleblower Scheme, or through the optional Speak-Up Contact | Policy, handbook, manager briefing |
| What happens after a report? | Acknowledgment within 7 days, assessment within 5 working days, feedback within 3 months, retaliation checks at 30/90/180 days | Procedure, templates |
| How is confidentiality protected? | Need-to-know access, restricted folder, redaction, no personal mailboxes | Procedure, access matrix |
| How do you know it works? | Annual review, sample case test, metrics | Annual review |
| What happens at 50 employees? | We implement the full §9 scheme before crossing the threshold | Trigger table |
