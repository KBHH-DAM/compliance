# Manager Briefing — Whistleblowing

**Audience:** Anyone who manages people or may receive a report
**Duration:** 10-minute read / 30-minute session
**Owner:** Compliance Lead
**Review:** Annually

---

## 1. Your role in one sentence

**Listen, protect, escalate — do not investigate alone.**

## 2. First 10 minutes

1. **Thank the person** for speaking up.
2. **Do not promise absolute confidentiality.** Say: "I will keep this to the people who need to know."
3. **Explain the routes:** the National Whistleblower Scheme (`whistleblower.dk`, 33 19 32 02) or internal handling by Data & More.
4. **Ask what they want:** to report externally, to have Data & More handle it, or to talk it through first.
5. **Take notes in their words.** Do not cross-examine, speculate or promise outcomes.
6. **Ask about evidence** and advise them not to investigate or delete anything.
7. **Tell them what happens next** — the Speak-Up Contact will acknowledge within 7 days.
8. **Escalate the same day** to [Compliance Lead]. If the concern involves them, escalate to [Chair].

## 3. Do not

- investigate the matter yourself;
- contact the accused, witnesses or colleagues before the assessment;
- tell the person they are wrong or that it is not serious;
- promise that no one else will find out;
- delete, alter or move evidence;
- discuss the case in open areas, chat groups or unsecured email;
- treat the reporter differently afterwards.

## 4. What non-retaliation means in practice

After a report, do not:

- change duties, shifts, pay, benefits or training without a documented business reason;
- give a negative review or reference without a documented, independent reason;
- exclude the person from meetings, projects or information;
- gossip or allow others to;
- delay or block a promotion, transfer or leave request without a documented reason.

If you need to make a legitimate management decision involving the reporter, document the reason **before** you act and tell the Speak-Up Contact.

## 5. Conflicts of interest

Hand the case to the backup or the Board contact if:

- you are the subject of the report;
- you are close to the subject or the reporter;
- you have a financial or personal interest;
- you have already formed a view.

## 6. If the reporter wants to stay anonymous

- Do not try to identify them.
- Record only what they chose to share.
- Explain that anonymous reports may limit what Data & More can do.
- Still escalate and preserve the information.

## 7. If the report concerns a data breach or security incident

Tell the Speak-Up Contact immediately. The GDPR breach procedure and the security incident procedure may also apply. Keep the whistleblowing file separate from the incident file.

## 8. What to say

> "Thank you for telling me. I'm not going to investigate this myself. You have two routes: the National Whistleblower Scheme, which is independent of us, or an internal handling by Data & More. I can help you contact the scheme if you want. Either way, you are protected from retaliation. I'll make sure the right person picks this up today."
