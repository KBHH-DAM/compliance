## DETAILED GAP ANALYSIS

### 🔴 CRITICAL GAPS (Operational Controls Missing)

These GDPR articles define processor responsibilities but lack dedicated ISAE control descriptions:

#### 1. **Art 31 - Cooperation with Supervisory Authority**
- **GDPR Requirement:** Processor must cooperate with supervisory authority and fulfill its obligations
- **Why Missing:** Not explicitly covered as standalone control
- **Related ISAE Controls:** Implicit in I.1, I.4 (breach notification cooperation)
- **Recommendation:** Could add as extension to **I.1** or **I.4**
- **GDPR Description from Cyberday:** "Processor must cooperate with authority on request, provide information, permit access"

#### 2. **Art 36 - Prior Consultation**
- **GDPR Requirement:** Processor must inform controller and consult when high-risk processing before processing
- **Why Missing:** Consultation mechanism not explicitly covered
- **Related ISAE Controls:** Implicit in **B.2** (risk assessment discussion)
- **Recommendation:** Could add as extension to **B.2**
- **GDPR Description from Cyberday:** "When processing has high risk, processor must conduct DPIA and consult before processing"

---

### 🟡 MODERATE GAPS (Partially Covered, Need Enhancement)

#### 1. **Art 12 - Transparent Information & Modalities**
- **Current Coverage:** **H.1** (procedure exists)
- **Gap:** Transparency mechanism not fully detailed (timing, format, accessibility)
- **Enhancement Needed:** Add requirements for:
  - Clear, understandable language
  - Concise and transparent information
  - Readily accessible information
  - Accessibility for persons with disabilities
- **Recommendation:** Expand **H.1** description

#### 2. **Art 19 - Notification Obligation (Rectification/Erasure)**
- **Current Coverage:** Partially in **H.2** (rectification/erasure)
- **Gap:** Notification to recipients about corrections/erasure not explicit
- **Enhancement Needed:** Processor must assist with notifying third parties who have received data
- **Recommendation:** Add as subset of **H.2**

#### 3. **Art 47 - Binding Corporate Rules (BCR)**
- **Current Coverage:** Mentioned in **G.3** (transfer mechanisms)
- **Gap:** Processor does not typically create BCRs; controller does
- **Note:** Only relevant for very large processors with group structure
- **Recommendation:** Reference as alternative to SCC in **G.3**

#### 4. **Art 48 - Unlawful Transfers**
- **Current Coverage:** Related to **A.3** (reporting unlawful instructions)
- **Gap:** Handling processor refusal to comply with unlawful disclosure requests
- **Enhancement Needed:** Procedure for refusing unlawful requests from third parties/authorities
- **Recommendation:** Could extend **A.3** for non-controller requests

#### Regulatory/Compliance (Not Operational Control)
- **Art 83** (Administrative fines/penalties)

#### Data Categories (Classification Policy, Not Procedure)
